# VDP Phase 1 diagnostic captures

The UART and HDMI diagnostics used RAM-only TFTP images before the final image.
Offsets below are relative to VDP MMIO at 0xf8cc0000.

## Color path

Factory MV320 xdp_drv_set_vid_ifir at c05e83f8 writes mode 3 for V0 and loads
eight signed taps. The factory taps at c107f3c0 are
-7, 12, -17, 25, -38, 58, -105, 328. The four packed registers read
000c03f9, 001903ef, 003a03da, 01480397 at 0x2604 through 0x2610.

With Y=400, Cb=700 and Cr=600 injected as 10-bit codes, the V0 layer
mute register 0x2098 and VP0 mute register 0x4098 both gave RGB (146,59,219).
FDR mute via 0x8200 and 0x8214 gave RGB (0,177,1). The injected FDR result
was also green with the vertical FIR disabled and without the IFIR fix.

With factory IFIR and the experimental ZME FIR, the 1080p capture on the
2160p link depended on position. At (960,540), the reference RGB MAE was
84.36/255. Setting (960,0) gave 3.07/255. At the same centered position,
clearing the ZME1 vertical chroma FIR bit 23 in 0x22d8 and leaving the
vertical scaler enabled gave 3.10/255 even with the chroma LUT address
cleared. The 4K capture gave 2.93/255 in that configuration. These
diagnostic MAEs are from 160x90 Pillow-resized crops.

## VT1 and asynchronous scanout

On the early asynchronous image, both 4K 240-frame streams completed
with seven CAPTURE buffers, 239 missed vblanks and 30.00 FPS.
Short 1080p tests at VT1 line 2249 repeated the 30 FPS cadence.
VT1 at 12 gave approximately 60 FPS with no missed vblanks, with
HD0_STATE bit 1 reporting blanking. The final image retains line 12.

The sequential 240-frame Main and Main10 inputs use FFmpeg testsrc2 at
3840x2160 and 60 fps. Encode one closed I/P GOP with libx265 ultrafast and
pools=8:frame-threads=4:wpp=0:bframes=0:ref=2:keyint=250:min-keyint=250:scenecut=0:open-gop=0.
Use yuv420p or yuv420p10le and prepare the request stream and manifest with
scripts/vdec_hevc_fixture.py --no-reference.
